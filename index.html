How Nandha Lost & Found Works

Built specifically for students, staff, and campus security of Nandha Engineering College.

01

Report Your Item

Submit item details, specify the block or court where you last saw it, and optionally attach a photo.

02

Campus Matching

Our platform automatically compares categories, descriptions, locations, and timeframes to find matches.

03

Security Verification

Found items are deposited at Block 1 Security Gate or the respective department for safe-keeping.

04

Collect & Handover

Present your student college ID card, verify unique identifying marks, and safely recover your item.

Campus Lost & Found Protocol

Official procedure for students and staff at Nandha Engineering College, Erode

1

Online Registration

Students submit a lost or found report with exact block locations (e.g. Block 1, Cafeteria, Basketball Court). An official report ID is assigned.

2

Automated Proximity Matching

The platform correlates item categories, dates, descriptions, and campus spots. When a match score exceeds 70%, both parties receive an indicator.

3

Physical Custody at Security Control

Found valuables (wallets, laptops, phones, cards) are held in the Central Security Control Room at Block 1 Ground Floor.

4

Claim Verification & Release

The owner verifies identifying marks, provides their student ID, signs the handover register, and the status updates to 'Returned'.
